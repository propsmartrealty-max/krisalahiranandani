A Krisala × Hiranandani Masterpiece

The Sanctuaries of Sector Arcadia & Sector Icon

Everlyn represents the residential pinnacle of the 105-acre Krisala × Hiranandani Township in North Hinjewadi (Darumbre). Designed under Hiranandani's neoclassical architectural philosophy and constructed using high-precision Mivan aluminium formwork, Everlyn delivers homes engineered for generations.

Encompassing two signature residential sectors—Sector Arcadia and Sector Icon—the residences range from thoughtfully configured 2 BHK and 3 BHK family homes to grand 4 BHK residences and signature double-height duplex penthouses overlooking the 8-acre private racecourse.

Surrounded by lotus ponds, biofiltration wetlands, and vehicular-free landscaped podiums, Everlyn offers an oasis of serenity just 8 to 12 minutes from Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park Phase 1, 2, and 3.

  • Sector Arcadia: Premium 2 & 3 BHK apartments for modern tech families.
  • Sector Icon: Ultra-luxury 3 & 4 BHK residences and bespoke duplexes.
  • Zero-Traffic Podium: 100% vehicular-free podium for children and senior wellness.
  • Racecourse Panorama: Unobstructed views of the private equestrian track and Sahyadri greens.

Frequently Answered Queries

Everlyn Residences FAQ

What configurations are available in Everlyn?

Everlyn offers 2 BHK and 3 BHK residences in Sector Arcadia, and 3 BHK, 4 BHK, and double-height duplex residences in Sector Icon.

What is the starting price for 2 BHK in Sector Arcadia?

Indicative pricing for a 2 BHK residence in Sector Arcadia starts from ₹79 Lakhs* onwards, with flexible construction-linked payment plans.

What construction technology is used?

All residential towers are engineered using high-precision Mivan aluminium formwork monolithic concrete construction, ensuring earthquake resistance, superior acoustic insulation, and zero seepage.

How far is Everlyn from Hinjewadi IT Park?

Everlyn is located in North Hinjewadi (Darumbre), approximately 8 to 12 minutes from Hinjewadi Phase 1, Phase 2, and Phase 3 IT hubs, with direct access to the upcoming Metro Line 3 station.
