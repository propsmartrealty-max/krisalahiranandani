Why do Infosys and TCS employees prefer living on the Marunji–Darumbre side over Wakad?
Wakad and Balewadi commuters must cross the congested Bhumkar Chowk and Shivaji Chowk highway bridges twice daily. North Hinjewadi / Darumbre approaches Phase 2 and Phase 3 from the uncongested western side.
What rental yield do 2 BHK flats earn from Hinjewadi IT professionals?
2 BHK luxury flats inside institutional neoclassical townships command ₹30,000–₹36,000/month from senior tech couples and GCC managers, generating 4.8%–5.4% gross yields.
