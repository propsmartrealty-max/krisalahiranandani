  Common Questions

Sector Icon FAQ

Answers to the most frequently asked questions about Sector Icon residences

What makes the Presidential Duplex at Sector Icon truly unique?
The Presidential Duplex at Sector Icon is designed as a two-storey private home within Tower E's upper floors. The defining feature is the extraordinary 18-foot double-height living room — a volume of light and space unprecedented in Pune residential architecture. This connects to an open mezzanine sky lounge on the upper floor, accessed via a private floating staircase clad in Italian marble. Each Duplex has its own private lift lobby (shared with at most one neighbour), a private racecourse-facing sundeck, spa-grade master bathroom, and walk-in wardrobe suite. It is, in essence, a bungalow in the sky.
What is the all-inclusive price range for Sector Icon apartments?
Indicative pricing at Sector Icon starts from approximately ₹1.85 Cr* for the 3 BHK Grande (Tower D, 1,250–1,450 sq.ft.), ₹2.85 Cr* for the 4 BHK Ultra-Luxury (Tower D & E, 1,850–2,300 sq.ft.), and ₹4.2 Cr* onwards for the Presidential Duplex (Tower E, 2,600–3,400 sq.ft.). Prices are indicative, subject to floor premium, preferred views, and availability. Contact our sales concierge for a comprehensive, all-inclusive cost sheet.
Do all Sector Icon units get direct racecourse views?
Racecourse-facing views are available in select upper-floor units of Tower D and across all Presidential Duplex residences in Tower E. Units on the south and west faces of Tower D above the 15th floor typically command the most direct and unobstructed sight-lines to the 8-acre private racecourse. We recommend registering your interest to receive a unit-specific view analysis with our sales concierge.
What is the expected possession timeline for Sector Icon?
As per MahaRERA registration PR1260002600818, the expected possession timeline for Sector Icon (Tower D and Tower E) is Q4 2028. Construction employs Mivan aluminium formwork monolithic concrete technology — a system known for precision, seismic resistance, and accelerated construction timelines relative to conventional RCC methods. Regular construction updates are provided to registered buyers on a quarterly basis.
How is Sector Icon different from Sector Arcadia within Everlyn?
Sector Arcadia (Towers A, B, and C) is the premium residential cluster within Everlyn, offering 2 BHK and 3 BHK homes with wellness-oriented amenities including lotus ponds, butterfly gardens, and a zero-traffic podium. Sector Icon (Tower D and E) is the ultra-luxury flagship cluster, positioned above Arcadia in both scale and specification. Icon offers 3 BHK Grande, 4 BHK Ultra-Luxury, and Presidential Duplex residences with Italian marble flooring, 18ft double-height ceilings, private lift lobbies, racecourse-facing sundecks, and exclusive Icon-only amenities including an infinity rooftop pool, cigar lounge, private theatre, and concierge service. The two sectors complement each other within a single masterplanned community.
