What is the starting monthly EMI for a 2 BHK in Krisala Hiranandani Pune?

What is the Stamp Duty and Registration charge in Pune for 2026?
In Pune (under PMRDA and PMC jurisdiction), the standard stamp duty for residential property is 7% (including 5% basic stamp duty, 1% Local Body Tax, and 1% Metro Cess). Registration fee is ₹30,000 for properties valued above ₹30 Lakhs.
