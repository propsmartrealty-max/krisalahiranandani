Krisala Developers & Hiranandani Communities Launch Landmark 105-Acre Township in North Hinjewadi, Pune

Pune, Maharashtra — November 2026

In an unprecedented collaboration reshaping Western India's luxury real estate landscape, Krisala Developers and Hiranandani Communities (under the visionary leadership of Dr. Niranjan Hiranandani) have launched their flagship 105-acre integrated development: [Hiranandani Pune](https://krisalahiranandanitownships.com/hiranandani-pune).

Strategically located on the PMRDA 128-meter Ring Road in North Hinjewadi (Darumbre / Marunji Boulevard), the township introduces: 1. India's First Residential 8-Acre Private Racecourse & Polo Club: Curated for equine elegance, featuring equestrian stables, showjumping arenas, and private racecourse pathways. 2. 40-Acre Della Luxury Resort Precinct: Designed in exclusive partnership with Della Resorts & Adventure, featuring bespoke villa plots and resort-style five-star privileges. 3. Neoclassical Roman Architecture: Iconic high-rise towers engineered with monolithic Mivan RCC technology, Roman archways, fluted Corinthian pillars, and 18-foot double-height grand entrance lobbies. 4. MahaRERA Statutory Sanctions: Fully registered under MahaRERA PR1260002500600 (Sector Arcadia), PR1260002600818 (The Colosseum Phase 4), and PR1260002502438 (The Della Collection).
