Township Overview & Architectural Excellence

The 105-acre Krisala × Hiranandani integrated neoclassical township in North Hinjewadi (Darumbre), Pune West, represents a master-planned collaboration between Krisala Developers and Hiranandani Communities. Engineered with precision Mivan aluminium formwork, TERI 50-year certified hydrology, and an 8-acre private equestrian racecourse, the township sets a new standard for luxury living.
