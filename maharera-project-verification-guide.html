What are the 5 essential documents to verify on MahaRERA before buying a flat in Pune?

Every Pune buyer should inspect (1) Valid MahaRERA Registration ID on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in, (2) Title & Encumbrance Certificate + Mahabhulekh 7/12 Satbara, (3) Sanctioned Building Plan & Commencement Certificate (CC), (4) Quarterly QPR Architect/Engineer/CA Certificates (Forms 1, 2, 3), and (5) Exact RERA Carpet Area excluding external walls and loading.

5-Step Buyer Verification Protocol on MahaRERA Portal

  1. Visit maharera.maharashtra.gov.in → Registered Projects → Search by Registration Number (e.g. PR1260002500600).
  2. Download Form B (Promoter Declaration confirming free-from-encumbrance title and RERA possession timeline).
  3. Verify RERA Carpet Area (strictly usable internal floor area + internal partition walls; balconies/terraces are listed separately).
  4. Inspect Litigation & Mortgage Charge Disclosures under Registered Encumbrances so there are zero undisclosed landowner disputes.
  5. Cross-check online Digital 7/12 Extract on mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in under Pune Division → Mulshi Taluka → Darumbre / Marunji.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does MahaRERA protect a buyer's token and booking instalments?
Section 4(2)(l)(D) of RERA mandates that 70% of all customer collections be deposited into a project-specific RERA designated escrow bank account, usable strictly toward land and construction costs verified by an Engineer and CA.
Why verify Authorised Agent MahaRERA registration (A031262401295)?
Under MahaRERA law, only registered channel partners may lawfully present allotments, preventing misquoting of carpet area, loading, or possession timelines.
