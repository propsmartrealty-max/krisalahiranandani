According to recent Maharashtra state land registration data, residential sales across Pune recorded a 21% Year-on-Year increase in 2025–2026, with premium 2 BHK and 3 BHK configurations accounting for over 72% of total transaction volumes. Corporate relocations, double-income tech households, and returning NRIs have created unmatched appetite for gated integrated townships with resort-grade amenities, air quality buffers, and signal-free highway mobility.

Legal & Financial Advisory

Pune Real Estate Financial Breakdown: Stamp Duty, GST & Ready Reckoner (2026)

Before committing capital to Pune property, buyers must accurately factor statutory transaction costs mandated by the Department of Registration and Stamps (IGR Maharashtra) and the Ministry of Finance:

  • 7% Total Maharashtra Stamp Duty: Comprises 5% Base Maharashtra Stamp Duty + 1% Local Body Tax (LBT) + 1% Metro Cess earmarked for Pune Metro infrastructure development.
  • 1% Female Homebuyer Concession: Properties registered solely in a woman's name receive a 1% stamp duty exemption, saving up to ₹80,000–₹1,50,000 on residential acquisitions.
  • ₹30,000 Government Registration Cap: For properties with agreement value above ₹30 Lakhs, the registration fee is statutorily capped at a flat ₹30,000.
  • 5% GST on Under-Construction Homes: Standard non-affordable under-construction flats incur 5% GST without Input Tax Credit. Ready-to-move homes with Occupancy Certificate (OC) attract 0% GST.

MahaRERA Compliance Checklist for Pune Property Buyers

Under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, Maharashtra was the first state to deploy stringent regulatory guardrails. Always verify these 4 mandatory elements before executing a sale agreement in Pune:

1. Section 4(2)(l)(D) Escrow

70% of buyer payments must be deposited in a designated project escrow account, legally ring-fenced solely for land cost and construction.

2. Carpet Area Certification

Agreements must be executed on net usable RERA carpet area rather than ambiguous super-built-up or salable area formulations.

3. Building Sanctions & CC

Verify Commencement Certificate (CC) issued by PMRDA / PMC along with structural engineer stability approvals.

Pune Real Estate: Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Pune real estate considered India's best property investment destination in 2026?

Pune real estate delivers high capital appreciation (14%-18% CAGR in growth corridors) paired with robust gross rental yields (4.8%-5.4% in tech belts like Hinjewadi). The city's thriving IT, automotive, and BFSI sectors, combined with major infrastructure upgrades like Metro Line 3, PMRDA 128m Ring Road, and the missing link on the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, drive steady residential demand from over 400,000 corporate professionals.

Which region is better for real estate investment: West Pune or East Pune?

West Pune (anchored by Hinjewadi, Mahalunge, Wakad, Baner, and Darumbre) consistently outperforms East Pune (Hadapsar, Kharadi) in tenant absorption speed and rental yields. West Pune properties yield 4.8%-5.4% versus East Pune's 2.9%-3.4%, driven by direct access to the Mumbai-Pune Expressway and the massive 3-phase Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park.

What is the average price per square foot in Pune real estate in 2026?

In 2026, residential property prices in Pune range from ₹6,500/sq.ft. in emerging growth corridors (such as North Hinjewadi and Darumbre) to ₹11,500–₹16,000/sq.ft. in mature zones like Baner, Balewadi, and Koregaon Park. Integrated townships like Krisala Hiranandani offer launch entry values starting at approximately ₹7,200–₹8,100/sq.ft., presenting significant pre-delivery capital appreciation potential.

What are the total government taxes and charges for buying a flat in Pune?

In Pune (under PMRDA and PMC jurisdiction), property transactions incur 7% Stamp Duty (comprising 5% base stamp duty, 1% Local Body Tax, and 1% Metro Cess). Registration fee is capped at ₹30,000 for properties valued above ₹30 Lakhs. Under-construction properties attract 5% GST (without Input Tax Credit). Sole female buyers receive a 1% concession on Maharashtra stamp duty.
