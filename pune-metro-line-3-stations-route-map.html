Which Metro Line 3 station is closest to Krisala Hiranandani Township in North Hinjewadi?

Megapolis Circle (Station 1 Terminal of Pune Metro Line 3) and Quadron Business Park (Station 2) sit just 4 to 7 minutes from Krisala Hiranandani Township along the Marunji–Darumbre spine, providing signal-free elevated rail transit across all 23 stations to Baner, University Chowk, Shivajinagar, and Civil Court.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Krisala Hiranandani Township from Megapolis Circle Metro Station?
Located just off the Marunji–Darumbre spine, residents reach Station 1 (Megapolis Circle, Phase 3) within 4 to 7 minutes—giving immediate first-stop boarding seat availability on trains heading toward Baner and Shivajinagar.
Will Pune Metro Line 3 boost rental yields in North Hinjewadi?
Yes. Following historical Pune Aqua Line appreciation around Kalyani Nagar and Vanaz, proximity within 10 minutes of Line 3 terminal stations boosts gross residential rental demand by 18–25% for 2 & 3 BHK executive apartments.
Does Civil Court connect Hinjewadi to Pune Railway Station and Koregaon Park?
Yes. Civil Court serves as Pune Metro's mega triple-interchange hub linking Line 3 directly to the Aqua Line (Vanaz–Ramwadi/Kalyani Nagar) and Purple Line (PCMC–Swargate).
