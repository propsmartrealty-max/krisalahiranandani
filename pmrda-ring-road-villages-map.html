Why is the PMRDA 128m Ring Road transforming Darumbre and North Hinjewadi real estate?

The 128-metre PMRDA Ring Road corridor places Darumbre and Marunji directly on Pune's premier signal-free orbital expressway connecting the Mumbai–Pune Expressway (Urse interchange), Chakan MIDC, Hinjewadi IT Park, and Balewadi/Baner without entering Shivaji Chowk gridlock.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Darumbre gain over interior Hinjewadi Phase 1–3 from the Ring Road?
Interior Hinjewadi pockets rely on single overburdened links through Wakad Flyover and Shivaji Chowk. Darumbre connects directly outward onto the Ring Road and Mumbai-Pune Expressway.
What does PMRDA Town Planning (TP) zoning mean for buyer safety?
PMRDA TP-sanctioned corridors mandate wider 45m–128m rights-of-way, proper storm-water gradients, and MahaRERA-escrowed infrastructure (PR1260002500600, PR1260002600818, PR1260002502438).
