Krisala Hiranandani Township Pune: The Complete Buyer's Handbook

Published: October 2026 • Reading Time: 9 mins • Author: Real Estate Intelligence Desk

The launch of Krisala Hiranandani Township Pune represents a landmark turning point in West Pune's real estate trajectory. Spanning across 105+ contiguous acres along the high-speed PMRDA 128m Ring Road in North Hinjewadi (Darumbre), this mega-development brings together two titans: Hiranandani Communities, legendary creators of Powai and Thane, and Krisala Developers, West Pune's engineering powerhouse.

1. The 105-Acre Master Ecosystem: A Class of Its Own

Unlike standalone towers in Wakad or high-density clusters in Mahalunge, Krisala Hiranandani Township is built on the philosophy of integrated neoclassical urbanism. Over 70% of the land parcel is dedicated to open green landscapes, internal 60-foot tree-lined boulevards, and self-sustaining social infrastructure.

  • The Colosseum (Phase 4): Roman-inspired residential towers rising B+G+27 floors, offering panoramic views of the Sahyadri mountains and internal racing turf.
  • Sector Arcadia: Smart neoclassical 2 and 3 BHK residences tailored for IT leaders and young professionals seeking acoustic isolation and laser-measured carpet layouts.
  • Sector Icon: Ultra-luxury sky duplexes featuring 22-foot double-height ceilings and private sundecks.
  • The Della Collection: 100 private equestrian villa estates (3,000 to 5,000 sq.ft.) curated with Della Resorts & Adventure.

One of the primary frustrations for IT professionals living in Hinjewadi Phase 1, Wakad, or Baner is the daily gridlock along Shivaji Chowk and the internal tech park bridges. Krisala Hiranandani Township eliminates this bottleneck entirely by positioning itself along the PMRDA 128m Ring Road.

  • To Hinjewadi Phase 1 (Infosys / Wipro): 10 to 12 minutes signal-free drive.
