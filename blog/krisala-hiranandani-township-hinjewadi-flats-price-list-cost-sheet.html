2. Maharashtra Statutory Cost Breakdown (Stamp Duty, GST & Registration)

When calculating the true acquisition cost of an apartment in Krisala Hiranandani Township Hinjewadi Pune, homebuyers must account for statutory charges levied by the Government of Maharashtra and the central GST council:

  • Maharashtra Stamp Duty (6%): Levied on the registered agreement value under the Maharashtra Stamp Act.
  • Pune Metro Cess (1%): Surcharge applied across PMC and PCMC administrative boundaries to finance Pune Metro infrastructure. Total standard stamp duty is 7%.
  • Govt Registration Fee (Flat ₹30,000): For all residential real estate transactions above ₹30 Lakhs in Maharashtra, registration is legally capped at ₹30,000.
  • Goods & Services Tax (5% GST): Applicable on under-construction RERA properties in the non-affordable category with zero Input Tax Credit (ITC).
  • Infrastructure & Society Corpus (₹2,50,000): Covers clubhouse development, electrical substation connection, water connection shares, and legal documentation.
