2. Mivan Aluminium Formwork Engineering Advantage

Standard residential buildings across Pune often utilize conventional clay or red-brick masonry, which introduces thick plaster layers, uneven room corners, and micro-cracking over time.

At Krisala Hiranandani Township Hinjewadi, towers in Sector Arcadia and Sector Icon are built using 100% cast-in-place monolithic Mivan aluminium formwork:

  • Zero Beam Obstructions: Clean floor-to-ceiling lines with maximum interior carpet efficiency.
  • Superior Seismic Resistance: Reinforced shear walls offering Zone III seismic protection.
  • Thermal & Acoustic Insulation: Monolithic concrete dampens sound transmission between adjacent apartments.
