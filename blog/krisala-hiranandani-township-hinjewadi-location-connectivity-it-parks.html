Krisala Hiranandani Township Hinjewadi resolves this challenge by occupying a master-planned 105-acre geographic footprint situated directly at the intersection of North Hinjawadi, Darumbre, and the Mahalunge smart city corridor.

By sitting outside the dense, congested inner streets of Hinjewadi Phase 1 and 2 while maintaining immediate arterial connectivity, residents of Krisala Hiranandani Township Hinjewadi enjoy pristine air quality, panoramic views of the Sahyadri foothills, and rapid signal-free transit to every major tech campus.

Two massive government infrastructure projects are fundamentally transforming capital appreciation at Krisala Hiranandani Township Hinjewadi:
