Why Hinjewadi Tech Leaders Choose Krisala Hiranandani Township Pune

Published: October 2026 • Reading Time: 8 mins • Author: IT Real Estate Research Desk

Over 400,000 technology professionals commute daily into the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park across Hinjewadi Phases 1, 2, and 3. For years, engineers and senior executives faced a difficult trade-off: live in cramped, noisy apartments in Wakad or Baner with a grueling 45-minute commute, or compromise on lifestyle quality in unorganized fringe developments.

The arrival of Krisala Hiranandani Township Pune in North Hinjewadi has fundamentally solved this dilemma. Here is why software architects, VP directors, and IT consultants from Infosys, TCS, Wipro, and Cognizant are choosing this 105-acre masterplanned sanctuary.

1. Reclaiming 2 Hours Every Day: The PMRDA Ring Road Advantage

Commute time is the single largest determinant of quality of life for IT families. By being situated directly along the PMRDA 128m Ring Road, residents of Krisala Hiranandani Township reach their campus gates without traversing the congested Shivaji Chowk or Wakad flyover bottlenecks:

  • Infosys Phase 1 Campus: 12 Minutes direct drive.
  • Wipro Circle Phase 2: 10 Minutes via Marunji arterial link.
  • TCS Sahyadri Park Phase 3: 14 Minutes via dedicated transit corridor.
  • Barclays Global Service Center: 11 Minutes.

2. Work-From-Home Acoustic Architecture

With hybrid work firmly established as the industry standard, residential engineering must cater to global video conferences and deep-focus productivity. Krisala Hiranandani incorporates:

  • Double-Glazed UPVC Sound Isolation: Blocks external environmental acoustic disturbance down to sub-35 decibels.
  • Monolithic Mivan Shear Walls: Eliminates vibration transfer and internal hollow cavity echoing common in brickwork towers.
  • Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH) Gigabit Readiness: Redundant high-speed connectivity channels pre-installed into every residence.

3. Rental Yield and Asset Appreciation (8.4% Projected Return)

Senior tech executives view home purchases through an investment lens. With Hinjewadi's Phase 4 expansion and thousands of high-earning software consultants entering Pune every quarter, tenant demand for gated lifestyle townships with sports clubs and clubhouses is unprecedented.
