PMRDA 128m Ring Road & Metro Line 3: The Infrastructure Catalysts For North Hinjawadi & Darumbre

How multi-billion-rupee transit investments place Krisala Hiranandani Township Hinjewadi at the geographic epicenter of Pune’s next economic cycle.

VY
Dr. Vikas Yewle
Principal Real Estate Research Analyst • Krisala Hiranandani Research Desk

Urban mobility is the cornerstone of sustained real estate appreciation. For tech professionals working in Infosys, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, and Tech Mahindra, daily travel fatigue on internal Hinjewadi village roads has long been a frustration. In 2026, the convergence of two major infrastructure corridors—the 128-meter PMRDA Ring Road and the Hinjewadi-Shivajinagar Metro Line 3—is permanently solving this issue.

1. The 128-Meter PMRDA Ring Road Alignment

The Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority’s 128-meter outer Ring Road is designed to divert long-distance commercial freight while providing high-speed radial bypasses between Pimpri-Chinchwad, Hinjewadi, and Mumbai.

Strategically positioned in North Hinjewadi (Darumbre sector), Krisala Hiranandani Township Hinjewadi directly abuts this 128m transport spine. Key commute milestones include:

  • Mumbai-Pune Expressway: Direct access in 10-12 minutes via bypass.
  • Hinjewadi Phase 1 & Phase 2 IT Parks: 15 minutes via signal-free express links.
  • Baner & Balewadi High Street: 18 minutes through the direct Mahalunge bridge.

Metro Line 3 Integration

The 23.3km elevated Pune Metro Line 3 terminates right at the Hinjewadi IT park terminal. Feeder transit and dedicated township shuttle services will connect residents of Krisala Hiranandani Township Hinjewadi to the metro station in under 10 minutes.
