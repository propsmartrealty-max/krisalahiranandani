In an era dominated by homogenous glass high-rises, Codename The Colosseum emerges as an architectural tour de force in North Hinjewadi. Designed as the flagship crown jewel of Phase 4 within the 105-acre Krisala Hiranandani Township Hinjewadi, The Colosseum marries ancient Roman monumentality with 21st-century Mivan construction precision.

1. Architectural Grandeur: The Roman Triumphal Archway

The entrance to The Colosseum is framed by an authentic neoclassical triumphal archway and double-height porte-cochère. The facade incorporates hand-finished fluted Corinthian columns, arched balustrades, and custom stone-textured moulding, providing an unmistakable sense of arrival.

The central amenity podium features a terraced Roman amphitheater overlooking manicured olive groves, outdoor screening pavilions, and a heated infinity-edge swimming pool with Sahyadri hill panoramas.
