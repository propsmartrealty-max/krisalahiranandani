Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) residing in the United States, UAE, United Kingdom, Singapore, and Australia represent one of the fastest-growing investor segments in the Pune real estate ecosystem. With the US Dollar trading at historical strengths against the Indian Rupee, overseas buyers command unprecedented purchasing power.

However, navigating regulatory compliance under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) and verifying Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority (MahaRERA) registrations is paramount to securing family wealth.

1. Statutory Title & MahaRERA Escrow Protection

Under Section 4(2)(l)(D) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, developers are mandated to deposit 70% of all funds collected from buyers into a separate scheduled bank escrow account, accessible solely for construction and land acquisition costs verified by chartered engineers and auditors.

RBI Guidelines & Repatriation Simplicity

Under General Permission from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), NRIs and OCIs do not require prior approval to purchase residential properties in India. Capital invested through NRE accounts is 100% repatriable overseas, with rental income repatriable up to $1,000,000 USD per financial year via Form 15CA and 15CB.
