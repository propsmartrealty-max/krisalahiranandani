What is Della Racecourse Pune in Darumbre, North Hinjawadi?

Della Racecourse Pune is a 40-acre equestrian and resort-living township (GDV ₹1,389 Crs / $150.1 Mn) inside the 105-acre Krisala × Hiranandani masterplan in North Hinjawadi, co-created by Jimmy Mistry, Dr. Niranjan Hiranandani, and Sagar Agarwal. It features an 8-acre turf racecourse and international polo club with 50 horse stables, 98 Private Luxury Villas & 5,000 sq.ft. freehold villa plots starting from ₹3.75 Crore*, 156 Della Branded Residences + 12 Sky Villas across three 18-floor towers, a 300+ room 5-star resort, Bespoke Private Offices, 8 signature dining venues, 1 Lakh sq.ft. Della Design District, MAYRLIFE European medical wellness, and 6 UN SDG sustainability standards.

What is included across the 40-acre Della Racecourse Pune township?

It integrates an 8-acre racecourse & international polo club with 50 horse stables, 98 Private Luxury Villas & 5,000 sq.ft. freehold villa plots starting from ₹3.75 Cr*, 156 Della Branded Residences + 12 Sky Villas across three 18-floor biophilic towers, a 300+ room 5-star resort, Bespoke Private Offices, 8 gourmet restaurants (including Saddle & Stirrup, The Imperial Turf Pavilion, Maratha Cavalry, Horseshoe, The Royal Derby, The Jockeys Club, Garden In The Sky), 12 wedding & 12 MICE venues, 1 Lakh sq.ft. Della Design District, MAYRLIFE wellness, Della Adventure Park, Senior Living, and 6 UN SDGs.

How far is Della Racecourse Pune from Mumbai and the Expressway?

Located at Darumbre, North Hinjawadi (Pune 410506), the township is 2.5 hours by road from Mumbai, 20 minutes from the Mumbai-Pune Expressway, 4 minutes from the PMRDA 128m Ring Road & Metro Line 3, and 10 minutes from Hinjewadi Phase 1–3 IT parks.
