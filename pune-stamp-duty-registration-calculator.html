What are the current stamp duty and registration charges in Pune in 2026?

In 2026 across Pune (PMC, PCMC, and PMRDA zones including Hinjewadi & Darumbre), standard residential stamp duty is 7% (5% basic stamp duty + 1% Local Body Tax/Zilla Parishad + 1% Metro Cess). Female sole homebuyers receive a 1% concession (6% total stamp duty). Property registration is 1% of agreement value capped at ₹30,000 for properties above ₹30 Lakhs, while GST on under-construction homes above ₹45 Lakhs is 5% (0% post-OC).

Frequently Asked Questions

Do women get 1% stamp duty concession on joint husband-wife registration in Pune?
No. Under Maharashtra IGR rules, the 1% Women Grahlakshmi Concession applies ONLY when the residential property is registered exclusively in the name of a single female owner (or joint female-female co-owners). Joint husband-wife registration attracts the full 7% rate.
Is GST applicable on Della 5,000 sq.ft. Freehold Villa Plots in Hinjewadi?
No. Under GST rules, sale of freehold non-agricultural residential land/plots after layout demarcation attracts 0% GST, saving buyers ₹18.75 Lakhs+ in tax compared to similarly priced built apartments.
What is the Metro Cess component inside Pune stamp duty?
Properties falling under PMC, PCMC, and notified PMRDA Metro corridors (including Hinjewadi Phase 1–3, Wakad, Baner, Balewadi, and Darumbre) include a 1% Maharashtra Metro Transport Cess on top of the 5% base stamp duty and 1% LBT.
