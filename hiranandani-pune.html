The Hiranandani Benchmark in Pune Real Estate

For over four decades, the name Hiranandani has represented the gold standard of luxury community living in India. Why Hiranandani Pune Delivers Superior ROI

Evaluating capital appreciation dynamics and rental yields between Mumbai/Thane townships and the new Hinjewadi equestrian development.

7.2% Rental Yield vs 2.8% in Powai

Hinjewadi houses India's largest tech corridor with 400,000+ IT engineers across Infosys, Wipro, TCS, and Cognizant. While Powai and Thane residential properties yield only 2.5% to 3.0%, Hiranandani Pune units generate over 7.0% - 7.5% gross rental yields driven by severe shortage of integrated township homes.

Ground Floor Entry Advantage

Powai and Thane townships trade at ₹25,000 to ₹38,000 per sq.ft. Hiranandani Pune launched at an early-mover foundational rate of under ₹7,500 per sq.ft., offering maximum capital runway as Hinjewadi Metro Line 3 and PMRDA 128m Ring Road complete commissioning.

India's First Private Racecourse

Unlike standard concrete clusters, Hiranandani Pune reserves over 70% of its 105 acres for open green spaces, professional equestrian dressage arenas, and 8-acre turf racecourse, establishing unmatched lifestyle moat and scarcity value.

Everything you need to know about the Krisala Hiranandani Township joint venture, MahaRERA dates, and booking procedures.

Is Hiranandani officially launching in Pune?

Yes. Hiranandani Communities, led by Dr. Niranjan Hiranandani, has officially partnered with Krisala Developers to develop a 105+ Acre integrated equestrian township in North Hinjewadi (Darumbre), Pune West. The development features neoclassical residential towers, Della luxury villa plots, and India's first private racecourse.

What is the starting price of apartments in Hiranandani Pune?

2 BHK luxury residences in Sector Arcadia start at ₹79 Lakhs all-inclusive, while 3 BHK premium apartments start at ₹1.25 Crore. Signature 3 & 4 BHK duplex penthouses in Sector Icon start at ₹2.10 Crore to ₹2.65 Crore, and custom Della Villa Plots start at ₹1.80 Crore.
