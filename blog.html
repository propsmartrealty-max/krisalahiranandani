The Hinjewadi & Mahalunge Mega Corridor: Why Pune’s West Is Outperforming East Pune (Hadapsar & Kharadi)

Pune’s real estate landscape in 2026 is witnessing an unprecedented geographic pivot. PMRDA 128m Ring Road & Metro Line 3: The Infrastructure Catalysts For North Hinjawadi & Darumbre

Urban planning history proves that infrastructure expansion drives 70% of real estate capital gains. North Hinjewadi (Darumbre) sits at the golden triangle of three generational civil infrastructure projects:

  • Metro Line 3 (Hinjewadi to Shivajinagar): Megapolis terminal station is just 2.5 km (4 mins) away, connecting residents to central Pune without touching road traffic.
  • PMRDA 128-Meter Ring Road: The Darumbre interchange sits 1.8 km (3 mins) away, allowing non-stop bypass travel to PCMC, Chakan auto hub, and Pune International Airport.
  • Mumbai-Pune Expressway Toll Plaza: Located only 3.2 km (5 mins) away, slashing commute times to Navi Mumbai and BKC down to under 90 minutes.
