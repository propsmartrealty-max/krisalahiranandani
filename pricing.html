  MahaRERA Compliant

Construction-Linked
Payment Schedule

The CLP plan ensures your payments are directly linked to construction milestones — protecting your investment and aligning builder accountability with financial commitment. MahaRERA mandated and fully transparent.

Why CLP protects you
  • No bulk upfront payments — cash flow friendly
  • Builder incentivised to complete milestones on time
  • Home loan disbursals align with each stage
  • MahaRERA verified & audited at each milestone
01
Booking & Agreement
10%
Paid at the time of booking. Locks in your unit, floor, and base price. Agreement of Sale executed within 30 days.
02
Excavation Complete
10%
Due when foundation excavation is completed and verified by the architect. Loan disbursement Stage 1.
03
Plinth & Foundation
7.5%
Upon completion of plinth level. The base structure that forms the foundation of your home is certified complete.
04
Slab-wise Progress (×8 Stages)
5% × 8 = 40%
The largest tranche — paid in 8 equal stages of 5% each as each floor slab is cast. Directly correlates to visible construction progress you can verify.
05
Brickwork, Plaster & Finishing
7.5%
Due upon completion of brick/block work, internal plastering, and major MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) installations.
06
Handover & Possession
15%
Final payment at possession — after OC (Occupancy Certificate) received, all snag lists resolved, and home ready for handover. Keys are yours.

Seamless Home Purchase

NRE / NRO Account Payments
Payments can be made via NRE or NRO accounts. Repatriation of funds from NRE accounts is freely permitted under FEMA.
TDS on Property Purchase
Buyer must deduct 20% TDS on payment to NRI seller. However, for new purchases from developers, standard GST rules apply.
Repatriation of Capital
Upon sale, NRIs can repatriate up to the original foreign exchange remitted or the sale proceeds (lower of two), subject to RBI guidelines.
POA & Remote Documentation
NRI buyers can appoint a Power of Attorney holder in India. We assist with notarisation, apostille, and embassy attestation.
NRI Home Loans Available
ICICI, HDFC, and Axis Bank offer dedicated NRI home loan products. Income in foreign currency is accepted with conversion norms.
