Where Thoroughbreds
Meet Towering Aspirations

For the first time in Indian real estate history, a township integrates a full-scale, operational 8-acre private racecourse within its living boundaries. The Krisala × Hiranandani Private Racecourse & Polo Club is not a future promise — it is the defining jewel of the 40-acre Della precinct, curated in partnership with Della Resorts and managed by professional equestrian management teams of international stature.

This singular address offers you something that no amount of money could historically buy in India — the privilege of waking up each morning to the sight and sound of thoroughbred horses on your private oval track, while the Sahyadri hills form an emerald backdrop. It is not simply an amenity. It is a way of life that entire generations of Indian affluence have been denied, until now.

A Private World Within Your World

The 8-acre racecourse at Krisala Hiranandani Township is not an amenity — it is an identity. In a world where luxury has been reduced to imported marble and branded hardware, we offer something that cannot be replicated, cannot be installed on the 47th floor of a tower, and cannot be delivered with a premium: the raw, visceral, overwhelming grandeur of a private racecourse.

From the duplex sun decks of Sector Icon, the racecourse unfolds like a private theatre. The distant rise and fall of a race, the chalk-white railing against the deep green of the turf, the controlled chaos of a Saturday derby — every resident experiences these not as a spectator, but as a protagonist. This is your track. These are your mornings.

The Social Season of a New Era

The social calendar of Krisala Hiranandani will be defined by a rhythm that India's most exclusive enclaves — from Delhi's Gymkhana to Kolkata's Royal Turf Club — have always known: the seasons of the horse. The Spring Derby. The Monsoon Cup. The festive season polo tournament. Evenings at the Della enclosure, champagne flute in hand, watching the final chukker of a sun-soaked polo match.

And on ordinary days — the yoga sessions in the saddle, the morning canters down the track, the equestrian training for your children. The normalisation of a lifestyle that the world's most rarefied families have built entire dynasties around. For the first time in Indian residential history, it is all within your gates.

The 8-acre private racecourse at Krisala × Hiranandani Township is India's first full-scale operational racecourse integrated within a residential township. Located inside the 40-acre Della precinct in North Hinjewadi, Pune, it features a complete oval track, international-standard polo grounds, professional stables accommodating 100+ horses, an equestrian training academy, spectator galleries, and veterinary support. It is curated in partnership with Della Resorts and expert equestrian management teams.
Access to the Krisala Hiranandani Private Racecourse & Polo Club is extended to residents through a structured membership programme. All township residents receive base-level access to racecourse events. Full membership tier details will be provided upon project launch.
The Polo Club at Krisala Hiranandani Township offers structured membership categories — Resident Member, Associate Member, and Patron Member — granting access to the polo arena, certified coaching sessions, seasonal tournament participation, and exclusive social events. Dedicated children's equestrian programmes are specially curated for the township's youngest residents.
