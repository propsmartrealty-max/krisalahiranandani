Frequently Asked Questions

Can Singapore permanent residents and OCIs invest in Della villa plots?

Yes, OCIs and NRIs are fully permitted to buy demarcated Non-Agricultural (NA) residential plots and apartments in India under FEMA guidelines.

How does the Singapore-India Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement work?

Under the Singapore-India DTAA, taxes paid in India on capital gains and rental income can be claimed as a tax credit against Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) tax assessments.
