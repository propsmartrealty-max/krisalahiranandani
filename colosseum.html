  About The Colosseum (Phase 4)

Where Purposeful
Design Meets Premium Living

The Colosseum (Phase 4) is the premium residential heart of the 105-acre Krisala × Hiranandani Township — conceived specifically for tech executives, young families, and discerning investors who demand more than just an address.

Spread across Towers A, B, and C, Arcadia offers an intelligently planned 2 BHK and 3 BHK residential ecosystem that seamlessly blends Hiranandani's architectural precision with Krisala's commitment to lifestyle excellence. With Mivan monolithic construction, IGBC green certification, and a zero-traffic podium, every dimension of Arcadia has been engineered to enrich daily life.

Strategically located in Darumbre, North Hinjawadi — the fastest-appreciating residential micro-market in Pune's western corridor — The Colosseum (Phase 4) places you 8–12 minutes from Hinjewadi Phases 1, 2 & 3, on a signal-free corridor with direct access to the upcoming Metro Line 3.

  Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about The Colosseum (Phase 4) — from configurations and pricing to construction technology and possession timelines.

What configurations are available in The Colosseum (Phase 4)?

The Colosseum (Phase 4) offers three distinct configurations across Towers A, B, and C: 2 BHK Luxury (740–820 sq.ft.), 2 BHK Grand Suite (820–890 sq.ft.), and 3 BHK Premium (980–1,180 sq.ft.). Each configuration has been designed with optimal space utilisation, premium finishes, and lifestyle-forward layouts.

What is the starting price of flats in The Colosseum (Phase 4)?

2 BHK Luxury residences in The Colosseum (Phase 4) start from ₹79 Lakhs*, 2 BHK Grand Suites from ₹92 Lakhs*, and 3 BHK Premium residences from ₹1.25 Cr*. Prices are indicative and subject to prevailing market conditions, applicable GST, stamp duty, and registration charges. Floor-rise and view premiums apply. Contact our sales concierge for a precise cost-sheet.

What is the expected possession date for The Colosseum (Phase 4)?

The Colosseum (Phase 4) is currently in active construction under MahaRERA registration PR1260002500600. The expected possession is projected by Q4 2028, subject to all applicable regulatory approvals. As a RERA-registered project, all timelines are legally binding, and unit-wise possession milestones are documented in the registered agreement.

What construction technology is used in The Colosseum (Phase 4)?

The Colosseum (Phase 4) uses Mivan Monolithic RCC (Aluminium Formwork) construction technology — the same method deployed in global premium high-rises across Dubai, Singapore, and Mumbai's finest developments. This technique delivers seamlessly smooth, crack-free walls, superior structural strength, faster construction timelines, and significantly better thermal and acoustic insulation compared to traditional brick-and-mortar methods.

How is The Colosseum (Phase 4) different from Sector Icon within the township?

While Sector Icon is positioned as the ultra-premium sky-living precinct — featuring signature 3, 4, and 5 BHK residences, exclusive sky lobbies, and iconic penthouses — The Colosseum (Phase 4) is designed for premium family living, offering intelligently planned 2 BHK and 3 BHK configurations that deliver exceptional value, design quality, and connectivity for tech professionals and young families. Arcadia is the ideal choice for first-time luxury home buyers and mid-tier investors seeking premium Hiranandani-standard construction at accessible entry points.
