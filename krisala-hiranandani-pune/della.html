THE DELLA COLLECTION

40-Acre Resort-Style Precinct • Ultra-Premium Villa Plots • Equestrian Luxury

A Partnership of Legends

Where Hiranandani Neoclassicism Meets Della Hospitality Innovation

The Della phase at Krisala Hiranandani Township represents a groundbreaking paradigm in Indian luxury real estate. Conceived in partnership with Della Resorts & Adventure (led by designer Jimmy Mistry), this 40-acre dedicated precinct fuses 5-star hospitality, bespoke estate living, and high-adrenaline equestrian sporting.

Centred around India's first residential 8-acre private racecourse and polo club, villa plot owners gain privileged access to a lifestyle once reserved solely for royal estates—all situated just minutes from Pune's bustling IT corridor.

The Visionary Collaboration: Mistry × Hiranandani × Krisala

Della Townships, Hiranandani Communities, and Krisala Developers have entered a landmark strategic partnership to develop the 40-acre Della Racecourse Pune district at Darumbre, North Hinjewadi. Frequently Answered Queries

The Della Collection FAQ

What makes The Della Collection unique in Pune?

The Della Collection is Pune's only integrated development featuring an 8-acre private racecourse, polo club, and 5-star resort hospitality managed in partnership with Jimmy Mistry's Della Resorts.

Can buyers construct customized villas on the plots?

Yes. Plot buyers have the freedom to construct custom villas aligned with the township's neoclassical architectural guidelines, with options for Della Resorts turnkey design assistance.

What are the infrastructure provisions for the villa plots?

All plots come fully serviced with underground electrical cabling, piped water supply, fiber-optic broadband, rainwater harvesting, sewage treatment connection, and 24/7 biometric security surveillance.
