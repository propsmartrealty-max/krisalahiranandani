Frequent Real Estate Queries

Comprehensive Krisala Hiranandani Buyer & Investor FAQs

Clear, transparent answers to help home buyers, NRI investors, and IT professionals evaluate the township.

Is Krisala Hiranandani Township worth buying for living and investment?

Yes. Krisala × Hiranandani Township combines Hiranandani's renowned master-township legacy with Krisala's execution excellence. With a 105-acre master parcel, India's first residential private racecourse, Della hospitality precinct, and proximity to Hinjewadi IT Park (8–10 mins), it represents one of West Pune's highest capital appreciation and rental yield corridors.

Where exactly is Krisala Hiranandani Township located?

The township is located at North Hinjawadi, Darumbre, Pune, Maharashtra 410506 (Geographic coordinates: 18.5913° N, 73.7389° E). It enjoys signal-free connectivity to Hinjewadi Phases 1, 2, and 3, Wakad, Punawale, and the Mumbai-Pune Expressway.

Who are the developers behind this integrated project?

The project is developed under a strategic partnership between Krisala Developers (Wakad, Pune) and Hiranandani Communities (Mumbai/Pune), bringing together premier neoclassical architecture, sustainable urban planning, and advanced Mivan construction engineering.

What configurations and residences are offered in Phase 3?

Phase 3 offers 2 BHK and 3 BHK luxury residences in Sector Arcadia, and ultra-luxury 3 BHK, 4 BHK, and bespoke 3 & 4 BHK Duplex Residences in Sector Icon. Additionally, the Della precinct features exclusive villa plots.

What is the difference between Sector Arcadia and Sector Icon?

Sector Arcadia is designed for modern families and tech executives focusing on premium 2 and 3 BHK residences with smart lifestyle amenities. Sector Icon is the ultra-luxury flagship enclave offering expansive 3 BHK, 4 BHK, and signature double-height duplex homes with panoramic views of the racecourse and green valley.

What is the expected starting price and payment plan?

Indicative prices start from approximately ₹79 Lakhs* for 2 BHK residences, ₹1.25 Cr* for 3 BHK homes, and ₹2.10 Cr* for 4 BHK & Duplex units. Flexible construction-linked payment (CLP) plans with approvals from leading banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis) are available. All prices are dynamic and verified against MahaRERA registered inventory.

Are duplex residences available in Krisala Hiranandani?

Yes. Sector Icon specifically features premium 3 BHK and 4 BHK duplex residences featuring double-height living ceilings, private sundecks overlooking the 8-acre racecourse, and grand master suites.

What is the Della collaboration and racecourse precinct?

The township integrates a 40-acre resort-style precinct curated with Della Resorts & Adventure, featuring luxury hospitality, adventure zones, an 8-acre private racecourse, and an international polo club. This hospitality-led lifestyle amenity is distinct from the residential towers.

How far is the township from Hinjewadi IT Park and major tech employers?

The township is approximately 8 to 12 minutes from Hinjewadi Phase 1, Phase 2, and Phase 3, home to tech giants including Infosys, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, Tech Mahindra, Capgemini, and Barclays.

What is the status of metro and arterial road connectivity?

The upcoming Hinjewadi–Shivajinagar Metro Line 3 station is located within 8 minutes of the township. In addition, the Mumbai-Pune Expressway exit is 5–7 minutes away, and the proposed PMRDA Ring Road will provide seamless bypass connectivity across West Pune.

Is Krisala Hiranandani a good rental investment for IT professionals?

Yes. With over 400,000 IT professionals employed in the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park, tenant demand for quality gated communities with lifestyle amenities is consistently high. Projected gross rental yields in North Hinjewadi range between 4.2% and 5.0% annually.

What is the MahaRERA registration status?

The project is registered with the Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority (MahaRERA). Registered details, sanctioned layout plans, and carpet area certificates are available on the official MahaRERA website at maharera.mahaonline.gov.in.

How does Krisala Hiranandani compare against other Hinjewadi townships?

Unlike conventional high-density developments, Krisala × Hiranandani offers a low-density 105-acre integrated equestrian layout with an 8-acre racecourse, neoclassical architecture, 100+ amenities, and Della resort hospitality, providing superior long-term asset value and lifestyle prestige.

What are the facilities for children, families, and senior citizens?

The master plan includes dedicated multi-generational zones: zero-traffic children's play gardens, senior citizen reflexology pathways and shaded gazebos, pet parks, indoor gaming lounges, daycare and learning centers, and 24/7 emergency medical support.

How can NRI investors purchase property in Krisala Hiranandani?

We provide a dedicated NRI concierge offering virtual 3D site tours, digital KYC verification, transparent NRE/NRO banking facilitation, Power of Attorney (POA) documentation, and end-to-end rental management services upon possession.
